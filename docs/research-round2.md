# Party Line — Visual Research, Round Two: Conversation Layouts

**Date:** 2026-09-24 · **Method:** image-search CLI with quoted-phrase + UI/interface/screenshot/window/layout query formulas.
**Verification:** every entry was downloaded and individually eyeballed before inclusion. Honest labels are baked into the titles — later captures and modern photos of vintage hardware are labeled as such, never passed off as period screenshots.
